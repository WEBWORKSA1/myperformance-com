    Cookie Policy

    Last updated: September 2026

    Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature. This page lists what we use and how to control it. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

    What we use

    CategoryExamplesPurposeSet by
    Essential / preferencesmp-theme, mp-cookie, mp-modal, mp-subscribedRemember your light/dark theme, that you dismissed the cookie notice or newsletter pop-upMyPerformance.com (local storage)
    Tool datamp-habits, mp-focusStore your habit tracker and focus timer data on your device onlyMyPerformance.com (local storage)
    Analytics_ga, _ga_*Aggregate usage statistics to improve the siteGoogle Analytics (if enabled)
    AdvertisingIDE, __gads, __gpi, test_cookieServe and measure ads, including personalised ads based on your interestsGoogle AdSense / DoubleClick and partners
    Embedded mediaYouTube cookiesPlay embedded videos; we use privacy-enhanced embeds where possibleYouTube / Google

    Your choices

    • Personalised ads: opt out at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info (US) / youronlinechoices.eu (EU).
    • Analytics: use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
    • All cookies and local storage: block or delete them in your browser settings. Tool data (habits, timer) will be lost if you clear local storage; use the export button in the habit tracker first.

    Consent

    Where required by law, we show a notice on your first visit and treat continued use after acknowledgement as consent to the categories above. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies and using the opt-outs listed.
