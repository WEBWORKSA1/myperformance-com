    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: September 2026

    MyPerformance.com ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. The short version: our tools run in your browser and send nothing to us; we collect personal data only when you deliberately submit a form; we use cookies for analytics and advertising, which you can control.

    1. Data we collect

    a) Information you give us

    When you submit a form (contact, newsletter, free performance plan, contest entry, job application, advertising inquiry, tool or video request) we receive the information you enter — typically name, email address, role, and your message. Forms are delivered to us by email via a form-relay service, and processed by our email provider.

    b) Information collected automatically

    Like most websites we, and third-party services we use, may collect device and usage data: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring URL, approximate location (city/country level), and cookie identifiers. See "Cookies and advertising" below.

    c) Information stored only on your device

    Interactive tools (habit tracker, focus timer, theme preference, cookie acknowledgement, newsletter pop-up state) use your browser's local storage. That data never leaves your device and can be cleared through your browser settings or the tool's reset button.

    2. How we use data

    • To reply to your inquiry or request, including writing a free performance plan.
    • To send the newsletter you subscribed to (you can unsubscribe in any email).
    • To run contests and pay prizes.
    • To consider job applications.
    • To understand how the site is used and improve it (analytics).
    • To display advertising that funds the site.
    • To comply with legal obligations and protect against abuse.

    3. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

    Consent (newsletter, marketing cookies, contest publicity), performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps (responding to requests, contests, applications), and legitimate interests (site security, basic analytics, responding to correspondence).

    4. Cookies and advertising

    We use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Google's use of data is described at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

    We may use Google Analytics or a privacy-focused equivalent to measure traffic. Embedded YouTube videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode where possible; YouTube may still set cookies when you play a video.

    Where required by law, we display a consent notice and you can change your choices by clearing cookies. Your browser also lets you block or delete cookies; some features may then not work.

    5. Sharing

    We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf (form relay, email delivery, analytics, advertising networks, payment processors for donations and prizes), with contest winners' consent for publicity, or where required by law. Donations are processed entirely by the payment provider you choose; we never receive card details.

    6. Retention

    Form submissions are kept as long as needed to handle your request and for a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Contest and application data is kept for up to 12 months after the contest or hiring decision unless a longer period is legally required.

    7. Your rights

    Depending on where you live (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. To exercise any right, use the contact form and select "Privacy request". We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. EEA/UK residents may also complain to their supervisory authority.

    8. Children

    This site is intended for adults in a professional context. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

    9. International transfers

    Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

    10. Security

    The site is served over HTTPS. Form data is transmitted encrypted. No method is perfectly secure; please do not submit sensitive information (health, financial account details, government IDs) through our forms.

    11. Changes

    We will post any changes here with a new "last updated" date.

    12. Contact

    Privacy questions or requests: contact page.
