    How it works

    Four steps, one month

    Set your goal (days 1–3)

    Use the SMART Goal Builder. It must have a number, a baseline and a deadline inside the month. Enter it with the form below.

    Track it (days 1–30)

    Use the habit tracker or your own method. Keep evidence: screenshots, numbers, a short log.

    Submit your result (by the last day of the month)

    Before/after numbers, what you did, what you'd change. 150–500 words. Optional photo or screenshot.

    Winners announced (first week of next month)

    Judged on clarity of measurement, size of improvement relative to starting point, and usefulness to other readers. Announced in the newsletter and here.
