    Choosing habits that actually move your performance

    Track inputs you control, not outcomes you hope for. "Close two deals" is a goal; "make 10 outbound calls before lunch" is a habit. Limit yourself to three or four at first — completion rate matters more than ambition, because a visible streak is what keeps you coming back.

    Habits with the highest performance payoff

    • A protected 90-minute deep-work block before opening email.
    • Writing down the day's top three priorities the night before.
    • Logging one achievement with a number each day — your self-evaluation writes itself.
    • A 15-minute weekly review every Friday.
