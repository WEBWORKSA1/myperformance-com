    Why SMART goals still work (when you do them properly)

    SMART has been around since 1981 and still dominates because it forces the two decisions people avoid: how will we know and by when. The builder adds two things the acronym leaves out — milestones and a scoring rubric — because goals with intermediate checkpoints are completed far more often than goals with only a final deadline.
