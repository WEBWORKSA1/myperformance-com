    OKR rules that keep the worksheet honest

    • Objectives are qualitative and inspiring; key results are numbers. If a KR has no number it is an initiative — move it down.
    • Three to five KRs per objective, and no more than three objectives for a person or team per quarter.
    • Score 0.0–1.0. Around 0.7 means you set the right stretch. Consistent 1.0s mean you are sandbagging.
    • Separate outputs from outcomes. "Ship the feature" is an initiative; "reduce churn from 4% to 3%" is a KR.
    • Check in weekly on confidence, not just progress. Falling confidence is the early warning.

    Individual contributors: read OKRs for individuals. For personal performance goals use SMART goal examples.
