    Filling it in: what each section is really for

    Summary frames how your manager reads everything else — lead with your biggest outcome. Achievements is where ratings are decided; use the action → result → impact pattern and put a number in every one. Challenges demonstrates judgement and honesty; choose something real that you handled well. Strengths and development shows self-awareness — name development areas before your manager does. Goals should tie to team priorities so your manager can say yes immediately.

    Length

    One to two pages. Managers skim anything longer. Three achievements with numbers beat eight without.

    Example achievement (before / after)

    Before: "Managed the client onboarding process."
    After: "Redesigned client onboarding, cutting time-to-first-value from 21 to 9 days (−57%) across 40 new accounts, which Sales now uses as a selling point."
