FAQ

Questions people ask before their review

Is everything really free?

Yes. Every tool, template and guide is free with no sign-up. The site is funded by advertising, sponsorships and reader support.

Do you store what I type into the tools?

No. Tools run in your browser. The habit tracker and timer save to your own browser storage only. Forms you deliberately submit (newsletter, audit request) are sent to us by email.

Can I use the templates at my company?

Yes. Templates are free for personal and internal business use. Please don't resell them. See the terms.

Is MyPerformance.com connected to any HR software company?

No. We are an independent publication. "My Performance" is used descriptively and we are not affiliated with any company using a similar name. Full notice.
