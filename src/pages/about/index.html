About

Performance should be measurable, improvable and yours.

Most people get one formal conversation about their performance a year, run on a form they did not design, judged on criteria they were never shown. MyPerformance.com exists to hand the tools back to the person whose performance it is.

What we do

We build free, browser-based tools that produce real documents — self-evaluations, goal statements, agendas, plans — and we write guides that explain how the best-run teams actually handle reviews, goals, feedback and focus. Everything is free, nothing requires an account, and the tools never send your work to our servers.

How we're funded

Advertising, sponsorships, occasional affiliate partnerships and direct reader support. Sponsored content is always labelled and never influences editorial recommendations. If you find the site useful, supporting it keeps the tools free and funds new ones.

Our principles

  • Evidence first. We prefer a framework with data behind it to a slogan with a book deal.
  • Specific beats inspiring. A tool that writes one good paragraph is worth more than a hundred motivational posts.
  • Fair to both sides. Managers and employees both get honest guidance — including the parts each side does not want to hear.
  • Private by design. Tools run on your device. We collect only what you deliberately send us.

Independence and naming

MyPerformance.com is an independent publication. "My Performance" is used in its plain, descriptive sense, and we are not affiliated with any company, product or software using a similar name. Full details in our trademark and copyright notice.
