Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature. This page lists what we use and how to control it. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

What we use

Your choices

  • Personalised ads: opt out at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info (US) / youronlinechoices.eu (EU).
  • Analytics: use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • All cookies and local storage: block or delete them in your browser settings. Tool data (habits, timer) will be lost if you clear local storage; use the export button in the habit tracker first.

Consent

Where required by law, we show a notice on your first visit and treat continued use after acknowledgement as consent to the categories above. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies and using the opt-outs listed.
