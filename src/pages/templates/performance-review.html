How to fill in this performance review template

  1. Gather evidence first. Pull the employee's self-evaluation, goals from last period, peer feedback and any metrics. Never write a review from memory alone.
  2. Rate each competency 1–5 using the scale at the top, and write at least one specific example per rating. If you cannot give an example, you cannot justify the rating.
  3. Balance the strengths and development sections. Two to three of each. A review with no development areas is not credible; one with no strengths is demoralising.
  4. Make the development plan actionable: a skill, a method (course, project, mentor), a date and a way to measure it.
  5. Agree the goals together in the review meeting; do not present them as finished.
