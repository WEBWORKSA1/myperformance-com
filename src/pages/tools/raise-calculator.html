Why one negotiation is worth more than it looks

    
Every future raise is calculated as a percentage of your current salary. A 5-point difference today is not 5 points once — it is 5 points on every paycheque and every percentage raise for the rest of your time at the company, and usually your starting point at the next one. That is why the 10-year gap in the table is typically 12–15× the first-year gap.

    
What a "good" raise looks like

    
Typical merit-increase budgets at large employers run around 3–4% of payroll, with top performers receiving roughly 1.5–2× the average and promotions adding 8–15% on top. If your ask is above 10% you will need either a promotion case, a market-rate case (external offers, salary data) or an exceptional-performance case with numbers. Our guide covers all three with scripts.

    
Real vs nominal

    
A raise below inflation is a pay cut in purchasing power. The calculator shows your real change so you can frame the conversation accordingly: "a 3% raise at 3.5% inflation means I earn less than last year."
