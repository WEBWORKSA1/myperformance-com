    Focus ratio: the metric nobody tracks and everybody feels
    
    
    Your focus ratio is the share of working hours spent on uninterrupted, cognitively demanding work — the work that actually shows up in performance reviews. Below 30% and you are mostly coordinating other people's work; above 50% is rare outside of protected roles. Improving it by even 10 points is usually worth more than any productivity app.
    
    
    The three levers, in order of payoff
    
    
    1. Meetings. Decline, shorten to 25 minutes, or convert to written updates. Most people can recover 20–40% of meeting time with no loss of outcomes.
    2. Interruptions. Batch messages into two or three windows a day. Each avoided interruption returns roughly 10 minutes of refocus time.
    3. Admin. Automate or template the recurring pieces; a single afternoon of setup often saves an hour a week forever.
    
    
    Then protect what you recovered with a deep work schedule and the focus timer.
