Rules for a one-on-one that people actually want

  • It is the report's meeting. Their topics go first; status can be handled in writing.
  • Never cancel, only reschedule. Cancelled 1:1s are the most common reason people say they feel unsupported.
  • Ask, then shut up. The best questions are followed by silence long enough to be slightly uncomfortable.
  • Write down commitments. Two lines at the end: what each person will do before next time.
