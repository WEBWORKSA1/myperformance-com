    The eight dimensions of work performance
    
    
    Performance frameworks used by large organisations tend to converge on the same handful of dimensions, whatever they call them. We use eight: Results (do you deliver outcomes?), Quality (is the work right the first time?), Ownership (do you take responsibility?), Collaboration (do others do better because of you?), Communication (are stakeholders informed and aligned?), Growth (are you measurably better than a year ago?), Reliability (can people plan around you?) and Focus (does your time go where it matters?).
    
    
    How to read your score
    
    
    • 85–100 — top-performer territory. Your risk is invisibility: make sure the evidence is documented. Use the self-evaluation generator.
    • 70–84 — solid. One or two dimensions are holding you back; fix the lowest first.
    • 55–69 — inconsistent. Pick one habit from your priority actions and run it for 30 days with the habit tracker.
    • Below 55 — something structural is wrong: role fit, workload, clarity or environment. Read 12 KPIs to track your performance and have a frank conversation with your manager.
